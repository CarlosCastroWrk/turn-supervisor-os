# Turn OS Design Language — "Field Calm"

Los's references (via Mobbin, Aug 19 2026): Starling, Lifesum, Tiimo,
Wealthsimple, Headway, Nike. Their shared DNA, adopted as Turn OS law:

1. **One truth up top.** Every screen leads with one big glanceable line
   ("6 need you · 4 ready to walk"). Big friendly numerals — the data IS the
   decoration. No dashboard-fancy charts; data without dashboards.
2. **Every group folds and carries its count** (Tiimo's `MORNING (2) ˅`).
   Summary always visible, detail on demand. Applies to boards, crews,
   unassigned piles, reports.
3. **The walk is a speed lane.** Approve = tap → confirm → "Approved ✓" →
   next unit. Two taps, never more. Nothing may slow the walk; celebration
   is a toast, not a modal.
4. **One blue + soft tints, white cards on a calm canvas, generous air,
   44px targets, 16px+ inputs.** Same tool, real identity.
5. **Nags are dots, not banners.** Attention signals live as quiet badges on
   the tab/row that owns them (e.g. backup due = red dot on More). The Home
   screen belongs to the day's work, period.
6. **Every action confirms itself** (Wispr's "added a word" pop): toast with
   the exact thing that happened + undo where reversible. No silent taps.
7. **Workflows are sacred.** Polish presentation, never re-choreograph how
   Los works a turn without his explicit ask.
